Mobile detailing FAQ

Quick answers about booking and preparing for your Kolos Auto Detailing appointment.

What do I need to provide for a mobile detailing appointment?

Please provide access to water and an electrical outlet at the service location. Kolos Auto Detailing brings the detailing service to you.

Which areas do you serve?

Our primary location is Wayne, New Jersey. Mobile service is also available in Totowa, Woodland Park, Little Falls, North Haledon, Clifton, Paterson, and Fairfield.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the live Google Calendar booking section to view availability, select a date and time, and complete your booking.

What should I do if my vehicle is very dirty?

Text clear photos of the interior and exterior to (973) 454-1205 before the appointment can be confirmed. This helps us review heavily soiled vehicles in advance.

What detailing services are available?

Available services include interior car detailing, exterior car detailing, full car detailing, paint correction, and mobile car wash service. See the package list for current options and pricing.
